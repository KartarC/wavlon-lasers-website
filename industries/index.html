Buying a fiber laser for a Canadian or U.S. fabrication shop

Choose the machine around the work you need to bring in-house. Industry labels alone do not determine power, table size or chuck configuration. Use a recurring job and a difficult part to compare the complete production workflow.

Which fiber laser should a job shop compare first?

Start with the jobs that recur most often. Compare ProCut for an open-table workflow, PowerCut for enclosed cutting with a pull-out table, and UltraCut for a dual-exchange-table workflow. Then confirm power, usable bed size, safety provisions and material handling for the proposed configuration. A higher power rating alone does not establish lower cost per accepted part.

Should I choose a sheet laser or a tube laser for structural fabrication?

Plate brackets and gussets belong in a sheet-machine review; tube and hollow-section parts need a tube-machine review. For tube work, confirm section size, weight, stock support and finished-part handling as well as cut geometry. Share drawings when both processes are needed so the quotation reflects the separate workflows.

How should an HVAC shop compare laser cutting with outsourcing?

Use a representative mix of flanges, brackets and panels. Compare the current delivered cost and lead time with an in-house estimate that includes labour, gas, consumables, loading, maintenance and financing where applicable. Include utilization and bending or finishing work rather than assuming the machine stays fully loaded.

What evidence should I request before buying?

Ask for a representative sample review with agreed dimensions, edge finish and downstream fit. Use the sample-cut evaluation worksheet to distinguish measured results from estimates. Confirm trial availability and file-sharing arrangements before sending drawings or material.

Can I buy for a facility in Canada or the United States?

Wavlon serves equipment buyers in both countries. Review the Canada purchasing guide or U.S. purchasing guide for location-specific planning. U.S. quotations are available in USD; offices in Great Neck, Long Island are available by appointment. Confirm delivery, installation, utilities and support scope for your facility in writing.
