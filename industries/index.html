Industries We Serve

Fiber Laser Cutting
Across Every Sector

From automotive tier-1 suppliers to custom fab shops, Wavlon machines work across the full range of Canadian metalworking industries. Here's where our customers run their machines.

Automotive & Transportation

Brackets, chassis components, body panels, exhaust parts, and structural reinforcements. High-volume, tight-tolerance cutting of high-strength steel and aluminum alloys.

AHSS / UHSSAluminumHigh VolumeP-Series / X-Series

Structural Steel & Construction

Connection plates, gussets, angle brackets, and flat bar components. Thick plate cutting up to 80mm on the X-Series. Tube and hollow section profiles on the T-Series.

Thick PlateStructural TubeX-Series / T-Series

HVAC & Mechanical

Duct flanges, brackets, damper plates, access panels, and casing components. Fast cycle times on galvanized and mild steel at light gauge.

Galvanized SteelLight GaugeHigh MixS-Series

Aerospace & Defence

Titanium, Inconel, and aerospace-grade aluminum components requiring tight tolerances. Low heat input fiber laser process minimizes distortion in critical parts.

TitaniumInconelAerospace AluminumP-Series / X-Series

Agricultural Equipment

Frame sections, guards, brackets, and wear parts for farm implements and heavy equipment. Mild steel and AR plate cutting with fast turnaround for regional dealers and OEMs.

AR PlateMild SteelOEM PartsP-Series

Custom Fabrication & Job Shops

High-mix, low-volume work across all materials. Quick job changeovers, flexible nesting software, and a wide material capability make Wavlon the natural choice for job shops.

High MixAll MaterialsQuick SetupS-Series / P-Series

Food Processing & Pharmaceutical

316L stainless steel for food-grade equipment — tanks, conveyors, hoppers, and enclosures. Clean, burr-free edges that meet sanitary welding requirements.

316L StainlessSanitary GradeClean EdgesS-Series / P-Series

Energy & Oil & Gas

Flanges, pressure vessel components, structural members for upstream and downstream applications. X-Series handles the thick carbon steel and stainless these projects demand.

Thick PlateCarbon SteelDuplex SSX-Series
