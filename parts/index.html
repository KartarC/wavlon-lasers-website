Common Wear Parts We Stock

All parts are genuine OEM-compatible. For standard consumables, order directly from our fiber laser parts store at Machinist's Vault. Contact us with your model number for specialty or unlisted parts.

Cutting Head

Protective Windows (Cover Slides)

Zinc selenide or fused silica cover slides that protect the focus lens. Check daily and replace at first sign of contamination or damage.

Replace: Daily inspection / as needed
Cutting Head

Cutting Nozzles

Single and double nozzles in 0.8mm to 6mm orifice sizes for all material types. Worn nozzles are a common cause of cut quality problems.

Replace: Every 8–40 hours depending on application
Cutting Head

Focus Lenses

F150, F200, and F250 focal length lenses for S, P, and X-Series cutting heads. Replace when power loss or edge quality degradation is observed.

Replace: Monthly inspection / 500+ hours
Laser Source

QBH Connector Protectors

Protective caps and cleaning kits for the QBH fiber connector. Critical to protect during any maintenance work on the cutting head assembly.

Replace: Keep spares on hand
Chiller

Chiller Filters & DI Cartridges

Deionized water filters and inline strainer elements for Wavlon-compatible chillers. Low DI quality damages laser module optics over time.

Replace: Every 3–6 months
Chiller

Cooling Water Additives

Algaecide and corrosion inhibitor packages for chiller water circuits. Prevents biofilm and protects copper and aluminum cooling components.

Replace: Annually or with water change
Motion System

Linear Guide Lubricant

Machine-grade grease for X/Y linear guides and ball screws. Using incorrect lubricant voids the motion system warranty.

Replace: Every 500 hours or per PM schedule
Electrical

Servo Drive Fuses

OEM fuse assortments for servo drive cabinets across all Wavlon machine series. Keep a fuse kit on site to minimize electrical fault downtime.

Replace: As blown / keep spares
Bed & Slats

Cutting Bed Slats

Mild steel and stainless slats in standard 25mm and 50mm pitch for S, P, and X-Series machines. Replace when bowing or heavy slag buildup affects part flatness.

Replace: Every 6–12 months in production use
