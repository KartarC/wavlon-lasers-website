Installation

On-Site Installation & Commissioning

A Wavlon field technician comes to your facility for every new machine installation. We handle everything from rigging and levelling to final calibration and first-part test cuts.

We coordinate with your electrician on power requirements before the machine ships so there are no surprises on installation day. All machines are pre-configured for Canadian 600V/3-phase electrical standards.

  • Rigging plan and floor prep consultation before delivery
  • Machine levelling and geometric alignment to ±0.02mm
  • Laser head calibration and focus height setup
  • Chiller and assist gas line connections
  • CNC software configuration and network setup
  • Test cuts across all programmed materials
  • Operator handover — machine running before we leave

Remote Support

Remote Diagnostics & Support

Many issues that used to require a technician visit can now be resolved remotely. With your consent, our engineers can connect securely to your machine's control system and diagnose faults, update parameters, and resolve software issues in real time.

Remote access is optional and operator-controlled — you initiate the session with a single button, and you can disconnect at any time. We never access your machine without your approval.

  • Secure, encrypted connection — operator-initiated only
  • Real-time fault diagnostics and error code resolution
  • Parameter tuning for new materials or applications
  • Software updates and firmware upgrades
  • CNC program review and optimization
  • Available Monday–Friday 8am–8pm EST
  • After-hours emergency line for critical production issues

Preventive Maintenance

Keep Your Machine at Peak Performance

Annual preventive maintenance visits reduce unexpected downtime and extend the life of your machine.

Annual PM Visit — What's Covered

  • Laser source output power verification
  • Cutting head inspection and lens cleaning
  • Linear guide and rack lubrication
  • Ball screw inspection and adjustment
  • Servo motor and drive health check
  • Chiller fluid level and filter check
  • Electrical cabinet inspection — connections, fuses
  • Software and firmware version check
  • Full geometric accuracy verification
  • Operator Q&A session

Daily & Weekly Operator Checklist

We train your operators on the maintenance tasks they should perform every day and every week — the small habits that prevent major failures.

  • Nozzle inspection and replacement schedule
  • Protective window check
  • Air filter status
  • Cutting bed cleaning procedure
  • Slag drawer emptying frequency
  • Chiller temperature and flow monitoring
