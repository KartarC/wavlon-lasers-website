Why Laser Welding

Laser Welding vs. Traditional MIG/TIG

W-Series Fiber Laser Welding

  • Zero spatter — no grinding or post-weld cleanup
  • Minimal heat-affected zone — near-zero distortion
  • Weld speeds up to 8 m/min
  • Runs on standard 220V power
  • Air-cooled — no water chiller cost or maintenance
  • 4-in-1: weld, clean, cut, braze with one machine
  • Operator learns in under 1 hour

Traditional MIG / TIG Welding

  • Significant spatter requiring cleanup
  • High heat input — frequent warping on thin gauge
  • Typical speeds 0.5–1.5 m/min
  • Requires consumables: wire, rod, tips
  • Ongoing gas and consumable costs
  • Single purpose
  • Skill-intensive — long training curve
