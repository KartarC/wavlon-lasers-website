PowerCut Series

3 kW – 12 kW · Fully Enclosed Fiber Laser

The PowerCut Series steps up from ProCut with a fully enclosed safety cabin and an automatic pull-out single-table design. Operators load and unload outside the cabin while cutting happens behind closed doors — better optical safety, dust/fume containment, and a noticeably cleaner shop environment. Higher acceleration (1.2 G) and traverse speed (120 m/min) yield faster part cycle times.

Key Features

Enclosed. Faster. Cleaner.

Fully Enclosed Safety Cabin — Complete optical enclosure keeps laser radiation contained. Better safety compliance for production environments.
Automatic Pull-Out Single Table — Table pulls out of the cabin automatically for loading and unloading — operators never reach inside during cutting.
1.2 G Acceleration — 20% higher acceleration than ProCut for faster in-cycle movements and shorter job times.
120 m/min Traverse Speed — Faster rapids between cuts reduce total cycle time on multi-part sheets.
BOCI BLT Laser Heads — BLT-310 (3 kW), BLT-421S (6 kW), BLT-442 (12 kW) for precise, adaptive standoff cutting.
Yaskawa Servo Motors (Japan) — High-torque precision servo drives on all linear axes.
ROUST Linear Guide Rails (Germany) — German-engineered guide rails for smooth, accurate travel.
Schneider & ABB Electrics (France) — European electrical components throughout the cabinet.
Cypcut / Hypcut Software — Windows-native CNC software with DXF/DWG/AI import and live monitoring on a 27-inch console.
FDA & CE / CSA Certified — All components and cables meet certification standards.
Why PowerCut

The Step-Up That Makes Sense

Full Optical Safety Enclosure

Meet Class 1 laser safety requirements without a third-party enclosure add-on. The cabin is integrated at the factory.

Cleaner Shop Environment

The enclosed cabin contains fumes, dust, and spatter — reducing contamination on nearby equipment and improving air quality for operators.

Faster Cycle Times vs Open Machines

1.2 G and 120 m/min means noticeably shorter job times compared to ProCut's 1.0 G / 100 m/min — especially on complex multi-part sheets.
