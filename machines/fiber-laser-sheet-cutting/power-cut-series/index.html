Full Optical Enclosure

Class 1 Laser Safety — Built In, Not Bolted On

The PowerCut's full-perimeter cabin meets Class 1 laser safety requirements right out of the crate — no third-party enclosures required. The sealed cabinet contains laser radiation, fumes, spatter, and noise, keeping your shop compliant and your operators protected.

Full optical enclosure on all four sides — no exposed beam paths
Laser-safe viewing windows with auto-darkening protection
Fume and dust contained inside — cleaner shop environment
Reduced noise levels vs. open-gantry machines
Interlock system halts cutting when doors open mid-cycle
Precision-Built Interior

Every Component Placed for Performance

The WL-3015F internal layout is engineered for rigidity, thermal stability, and serviceability. The crossbeam travels on heavy German ROUST linear rails. The cutting table and slat system handle full 3015 sheets. Everything is accessible via large service panels for fast maintenance.

Heavy steel plate welded machine bed — vibration damping for accurate cuts
ROUST (Germany) linear guide rails on X and Y axes
Eblun (Taiwan) helical rack and pinion — smooth, backlash-free motion
Techmech precision reducers on all servo axes
Full-access service panels on all sides
Cutting Head System

BOCI BLT Laser Heads — Auto-Focus, Auto-Calibrate

Every PowerCut ships with a BOCI BLT series cutting head matched to the laser power. The BLT-310 (3 kW), BLT-421S (6 kW), and BLT-442 (12 kW) all feature automatic focus positioning, IP65 protection, and built-in collision protection to keep the nozzle safe if it contacts material.

Auto-focus: no manual nozzle height adjustment between materials
IP65 rated — protected from fumes, slag dust, and moisture
Collision protection system — head retracts on contact impact
Optical path sealed and pressurized — no contamination of optics
Compatible with optional Precitec ProCutter upgrade head
Loading System

Automatic Pull-Out Table — Load Outside, Cut Inside

When a job finishes, the cutting table automatically slides out of the cabin for unloading. Load your next sheet outside — then slide the table back in and start cutting. Operators never reach inside an active cutting zone, improving both safety and workflow efficiency.

Motorized pull-out and pull-in — one-button table exchange
Full 3015 sheet (3080 × 1540 mm) loads from outside the cabin
Interlock prevents cutting until table is fully retracted and doors closed
Slat table design — easy slag removal and cleaning
Integrated front and rear access doors for large part removal
Electrical System

Schneider Electric & ABB — European-Grade Electrics

The electrical cabinet is populated with Schneider Electric and ABB components — PLCs, inverters, breakers, and contactors. Every wire is labeled. Every circuit is protected. The 380V / 3-phase / 60Hz system is wired for North American facilities with CSA-rated components and cables throughout.

Schneider Electric & ABB components — PLCs, VFDs, and breakers
CSA-rated cables and components for Canadian electrical compliance
380V / 3-phase / 60Hz — standard Canadian industrial power
Yaskawa servo drives on all linear axes — precise motion control
S&A water chiller integrated for laser source cooling
All wiring labeled with ferrules — fast troubleshooting and service
Fume Management

Zone-Based Smoke Extraction — Active Cutting Zone Only

Unlike machines that extract from the full table area, the PowerCut uses a zone-based extraction system that activates only in the region where the laser head is actively cutting. This concentrates suction at the fume source, dramatically improving extraction efficiency and reducing the load on your dust collector.

Zone smoke extraction follows the cutting head position in real time
Significantly lower CFM requirement vs. full-table extraction
Reduces dust collector filter load and extends filter life
Integrated extraction ports — connect to any industrial dust collector
Compatible with Wavlon fume extraction packages (optional)
Cutting Bed

Refractory Brick Slag Pan — No Splash-Back

Beneath the slat table, the PowerCut uses refractory brick lining in the slag collection pan. Unlike bare steel pans, refractory bricks absorb slag impact and heat, preventing molten metal splash-back onto the underside of sheets and dramatically extending the life of the pan and surrounding structure.

Refractory brick lining — rated for sustained high-temperature slag
No metal splash-back onto sheet undersides — cleaner cut faces
Extends pan structure life vs. bare steel alternatives
Easy slag removal via front-access drawer on 3015 frame
