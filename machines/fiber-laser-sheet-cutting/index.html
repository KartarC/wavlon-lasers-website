Open Frame · WPC Series

ProCut Series
3kW – 12kW

The ProCut is Wavlon's open-frame workhorse — a clean gantry design with full-width accessibility, ideal for mixed-material job shops and fabrication operations where operators need easy access to the cutting bed.

  • Open gantry design for unrestricted loading and unloading
  • 3015 and 4020 bed formats — handles oversized plate
  • MAX fiber laser source with FSCUT 2000 controller
  • Light curtain safety system standard on all configurations
  • ProCut UL variant adds ultra-large 6020 bed option
View ProCut Series → Request a Quote
Enclosed Cabin · WFC Series · Most Popular

PowerCut Series
3kW – 12kW

The PowerCut is Wavlon's flagship enclosed cabin machine — a fully sealed cutting environment with integrated fume extraction, automatic door operation, and a compact 3015 footprint built for production environments.

  • Full enclosure with automatic sliding door for operator safety
  • Integrated zone smoke extraction — no external ducting required
  • Sectioned enclosure panels for easy internal access & maintenance
  • Left-hand and right-hand door configurations available
  • CE-certified electrical cabinet with Schneider components
View PowerCut Series → Request a Quote
Dual Exchange · WUC Series

UltraCut Series
3kW – 20kW+

The UltraCut is built for heavy production — dual-pallet exchange tables for near-continuous operation, high-power laser options up to 20kW, and a robust frame engineered for structural steel and thick-plate cutting.

  • Dual-pallet exchange table — load while the machine cuts
  • Up to 20kW for thick stainless, aluminum, and mild steel
  • Monitoring cameras for remote process observation
  • Optional bevel cutting head for chamfered edge preparation
  • Safety light curtains and fume monitoring standard

Material Capabilities

Cuts Every Common Metal

All three sheet cutting series support the full range of metals used in fabrication, manufacturing, and structural construction.

Mild Steel
Up to 80mm (30kW)
Most common material. ProCut/PowerCut handle up to 35mm at 12kW; UltraCut up to 40mm at 20kW.
Stainless Steel
Up to 60mm (30kW)
Nitrogen-assist cutting delivers clean, oxide-free edges on 304 and 316L grades.
Aluminum
Up to 50mm (30kW)
High reflectivity handled by MAX fiber source. Clean cuts on 5052, 6061, and 7075 alloys.
Brass & Bronze
Up to 20mm (6kW)
Used in decorative, electrical, and marine fabrication. Clean kerf with minimal burr.
Copper
Up to 16mm (6kW)
Electrical busbars, heat exchangers, and cooling components. High-speed cutting with fiber advantage.
Galvanized Steel
Up to 25mm (12kW)
HVAC ductwork, roofing, and enclosures. Fume extraction recommended — standard on PowerCut.
