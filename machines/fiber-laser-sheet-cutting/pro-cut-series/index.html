ProCut Series

3 kW – 12 kW · Gantry Fiber Laser Cutter

The ProCut Series is Wavlon's entry-level workhorse — a gantry-style fiber laser cutter with an enclosed cutting head and beam over an open machine bed. Ideal for general fabrication shops and job shops that want dependable daily-driver cutting without paying for a full enclosure or shuttle table.

Built-In Features

Engineering That Shows

Every ProCut ships with the same tier-1 components. No upgrades needed — this is the standard build.

Electrical System

Schneider & ABB — European Grade Throughout

Every ProCut features a fully organized electrical cabinet built with Schneider Electric and ABB components — inverters, contactors, circuit breakers, and servo drives. CSA-certified wiring throughout for Canadian compliance.

Schneider Electric PLCs, inverters & breakers
ABB contactors & motor starters
Yaskawa servo drives — Japanese precision
FDA & CE / CSA certified components & cables
Safety System

High-Sensitivity Safety Light Curtains

Integrated high-sensitivity safety light curtains on both ends of the machine beam. Immediate E-stop response to any intrusion during active cutting — keeping operators safe without slowing production.

Dual-side light curtain protection
Instantaneous E-stop on beam break
Safe restart after clearance — no manual reset delay
Gantry Design

Enclosed Gantry Beam — Open Table Access

The ProCut's distinctive gantry-enclosed beam protects the laser optics and mechanics from fume and spatter while keeping the cutting bed fully open. Load sheet stock from any direction — crane, forklift, or manual — without opening doors or waiting on a shuttle cycle.

ROUST German linear guide rails
Eblun helical rack & pinion drive
±0.03mm positioning / ±0.02mm repeat precision
Unrestricted table access — no enclosure overhead
Cutting Bed

Integrated Precision Slat Bed

Heavy-duty slat bed welded into the machine frame — no flex, no alignment drift. Replaceable mild steel slats in 25mm and 50mm pitch. Scrap drawer keeps the cutting zone clean and reduces fume pocket buildup under the workpiece.

Welded-frame slat table — zero flex
Standard 3015 (3m × 1.5m) or 4020 (4m × 2m) beds
Replaceable slats — stocked in Toronto warehouse
Scrap drawer for fast cutting zone cleanup

Key Features

Built for Oversized Production

Ultra Large Format Bed — 4020 and 6025 frame sizes handle structural steel, wide plate, and formats standard machines cannot accommodate.
Up to 20 kW — BLT-860H cutting head delivers confident cutting up to 40mm carbon steel in a single pass.
Yaskawa Servo Motors — Precision servo drives across all axes maintain repeatability on long-travel gantry systems.
Reinforced Gantry Beam — Heavier-duty steel beam construction engineered for the longer spans of UL format beds.
Open Table Loading — Unrestricted crane or fork loading from any direction — critical for handling oversized or heavy plate.
