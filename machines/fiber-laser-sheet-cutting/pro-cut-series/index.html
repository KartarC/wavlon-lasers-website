Key Features

Built for Real-World Fabrication

MAX Fiber Laser Source — Industry-leading fiber laser source with high reliability and minimal maintenance requirements.
BOCI BLT Laser Heads — BLT-310 (3 kW), BLT-421S (6 kW), BLT-442 (12 kW) for precise, adaptive standoff cutting.
Yaskawa Servo Motors (Japan) — High-torque precision servo drives on all linear axes for consistent accuracy.
ROUST Linear Guide Rails (Germany) — German-engineered guide rails for smooth, accurate gantry travel.
Schneider & ABB Electrics (France) — European electrical components throughout — inverters, contactors, circuit breakers.
Cypcut / Hypcut Software — Windows-native CNC software with DXF/DWG/AI import, technology tables, and live monitoring.
27-inch Console — Large internal-design touchscreen console for easy operator use.
Automatic Calibration — Automated calibration reduces setup time and keeps accuracy consistent.
FDA & CE / CSA Certified — All components and cables meet FDA & CE standards; CSA-certified wiring throughout.
