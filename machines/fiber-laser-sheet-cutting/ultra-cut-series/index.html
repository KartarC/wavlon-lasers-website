WUC · Production-Line Ready

Engineered for
Continuous Production.

The UltraCut Series is Wavlon's flagship platform — a fully enclosed safety cabin paired with dual exchangeable shuttle tables, matched chiller, and industry-leading motion specs. Covers both the 3015 and 4020 frame in one platform.

Dual Exchange Shuttle Tables

Load while you cut — zero dead time between jobs. The only Wavlon sheet series with full dual-exchange production.

Fully Enclosed Safety Cabin

Class-1 optical enclosure, laser-rated viewing windows, interlocked access doors, and integrated dust/fume extraction.

BOCI BLT Laser Heads

Adaptive standoff, automatic focus adjustment, and rapid lens exchange — matched per power tier from 3 kW to 20 kW.

Both Frames Available

3015 (3080 × 1540 mm) or 4020 (4000 × 2000 mm) — one platform, two work envelopes, every power tier.

Highlight 01 · Continuous Production

Dual Exchangeable
Shuttle Tables.

Two pallets. One cycle. While the laser cuts inside the fully enclosed cabin, the operator stages the next sheet on the second table outside — completely clear of the optical path. When the cut finishes, the tables exchange automatically and the next job begins immediately.

The result: load and unload time disappears from your cycle. The machine runs the way it was designed to — uninterrupted.

01 · Cut

Cut inside the cabin

Sheet A is processed under the fully enclosed cabin with active fume extraction and full optical containment.

02 · Load

Load outside, in parallel

Sheet B is loaded onto the second table outside the cabin while the laser is still running — zero interference.

03 · Exchange

Exchange & resume

Tables swap pull-type. The next cut begins immediately. The operator unloads Sheet A for the next cycle.

Highlight 02 · cont.

Intelligent Monitoring & Light Safety Curtains.

Eyes on every cut. Guard rails on every load. UltraCut layers an electronic safety envelope around the operator — transparent inside, protected outside, without slowing the dual-exchange cycle.

01 · Inside the Cabin

Intelligent Monitoring System.

UltraCut features an interconnected dual-screen system that lets operators monitor cutting in real time. Internal HD cameras stream a live feed from inside the fully enclosed cabin to the operator-facing console — every cut, fully visible without breaking enclosure.

02 · Outside the Cabin

Light Safety Curtains.

Photoelectric light curtains form an invisible safety perimeter around the shuttle-table exchange envelope. If the beam grid is broken during auto-run, motion halts instantly — operators load and unload in parallel with confidence, and the dual-exchange cycle never compromises personnel safety.

Key Feature · Cutting Optics

BOCI BLT Laser Heads.
Precitec optional.

Four heads, one design language. Each UltraCut power tier ships standard with the matched BOCI BLT cutting head — precision adaptive standoff, automatic focus adjustment, and rapid lens exchange. Precitec head integration is available as an option for customers standardising on Precitec optics across their plant.
