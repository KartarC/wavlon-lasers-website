TubeCut Double Chuck

1,500W – 12,000W · Four Models · ⌀120mm – 360mm

TubeCut Double Chuck is Wavlon's entry-to-intermediate fiber laser tube cutting platform, built around a precision double-chuck design. Four models — TDC-120, TDC-160, TDC-240, and TDC-360 — cover round, square, and rectangular profiles on a 6,000mm bed, scaling from small job-shop entry points up to growing fabrication shop throughput.

Features

Built for Job Shops & Growing Fabricators

Auto-Centering Chuck System — Pneumatic self-centering chucks accommodate round, square, and rectangular profile tubes without manual adjustment.
Entry-to-Intermediate Scaling — Start with TDC-120 or TDC-160 and step up to TDC-240 or TDC-360 as diameter and throughput needs grow, on the same control platform.
6-Axis CNC Control — Simultaneous 6-axis control enables mitre cuts, saddle cuts, fish-mouth joints, and 3D bevel geometry.
Anti-Scratch Roller Supports — Polyurethane roller supports along the cutting length prevent surface scratching on polished or coated tube.
Semi-Automatic Loading — Semi-auto material loading system feeds raw tube into the chuck with minimal operator handling.
Remnant Length Detection — Automatic remnant detection minimises material waste from partial-bar cuts.
CypTube CAM Software — Dedicated tube nesting and cutting software with DXF/STEP import, automatic joint generation, and NC output.
Canadian Sales & Support — Local sales engineering, installation guidance, and spare parts support across Canada.
