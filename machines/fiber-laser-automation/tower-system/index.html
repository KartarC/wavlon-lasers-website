Tower Automation System

Automated Sheet Storage & Loading — Lights-Out Operation

Why Automate

6 Reasons to Add the Tower System

Transform your laser cutting operation from attended to autonomous.

24/7 Unmanned Operation

Run jobs overnight and on weekends without staff present. The system loads sheets, cuts, and sorts finished parts automatically.

Maximum Machine Utilization

Eliminate idle time between sheets. The tower feeds the next sheet within seconds of the previous job completing — no operator wait time.

Multi-Material Management

Store up to 10 different material types simultaneously. Each drawer is individually addressable with RFID tracking and inventory management.

Reduced Material Damage

Automated air-float conveyors move sheets without scratching. No manual handling means fewer surface defects on finished parts.

MES / ERP Integration

Full integration with Wavlon MES and third-party ERP systems. Real-time inventory levels, job scheduling, and production reporting.

Scalable to 10 Towers

Start with one tower and scale up as your business grows. Each tower adds 500 sheets of storage capacity and connects to the same control interface.
