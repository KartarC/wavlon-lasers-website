Optical System

Precision optics for industrial fiber laser cutting

The BOCI BLT series sets the standard for industrial cutting head reliability. Every unit ships pre-calibrated and validated for the power range of its paired Wavlon machine.

Capacitive Auto-Focus

Nozzle height follows the workpiece surface in real time — consistent kerf width on warped plate, no manual re-zeroing between jobs.

Anti-Collision Protection

Mechanical break-away mount absorbs impact before damage reaches the optical path. Head realigns and resumes without operator intervention.

Ceramic Nozzle Interface

High-temperature ceramic nozzle holder maintains dimensional stability at cutting temperature — extends nozzle service life significantly versus brass alternatives.

IP65 Sealed Body

Cutting debris and spatter stay out of the optical cavity. Lens replacement is a tool-free 60-second field procedure.

Real-Time Lens Monitoring

Integrated sensor detects contaminated or damaged protective lenses before cut quality degrades — prevents scrapped plate.

Core Technology

What makes the BLT head stand out

Six key engineering decisions that show up in cut quality and uptime — not just on the spec sheet.

Capacitive Height Sensing

The nozzle tip acts as one plate of a capacitor relative to the workpiece. Changes in gap distance produce a measurable signal that drives the Z-axis in real time — maintaining standoff to ±0.02 mm regardless of plate bow or warp.

Anti-Collision Mount

A precision spring-loaded break-away pivot absorbs lateral and axial impact before force reaches the lens stack. The head swings clear, limit switches stop the servo, and the operator re-seats the head in seconds — no optical alignment needed.

Dual-Lens Optical Path

Separate collimation and focusing lenses let Wavlon configure the correct focal spot diameter for each power class. Higher-power machines run a larger collimation lens to keep power density within the optical coatings' safe operating window.

Ceramic Nozzle Seat

High-purity alumina ceramic tolerates the heat and spatter load at the cutting zone without deforming. A consistent nozzle bore dimension is critical for gas-assist pressure and cutting-gas symmetry — ceramic delivers this reliably for millions of piercing cycles.

Protective Lens Monitoring

An inline backscatter sensor continuously measures transmission through the protective lens. A contaminated or cracked lens drops transmission below threshold — the control raises an alarm before the deterioration reaches the focusing optic or ruins a part.

IP65 Sealed Enclosure

Full dust and spatter exclusion without sacrificing quick lens access. The protective lens cassette snaps out and back in with no tools — designed for a 60-second field swap during shift changeover, not a maintenance window.
