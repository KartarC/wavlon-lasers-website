Feature Definitions

Technology Glossary

Plain-language explanations of every BLT intelligent feature.

Closed-Loop Monitoring
Built-in multi-sensor array provides real-time condition feedback, enabling rapid detection of abnormalities and early warning for improved cutting stability.
Fast Focusing
Motorised collimating lens adjusts focal position at up to 500 mm/s, enabling rapid changes when switching materials or plate thicknesses without manual intervention.
Built-In Capacitance Amplifier
Integrated amplifier improves sensing accuracy and height-following stability during cutting, improving precision in automated sheet metal applications.
Full-Body Water Cooling
Cooling channels cover 90% of the optical path, maintaining stable internal temperatures at higher powers and improving optical protection and reliability.
Collision Protection
Protective screw / cartridge mechanism absorbs impact when the head contacts material or fixtures, preventing serious damage and eliminating depot-repair downtime.
Protective Window Monitoring
Real-time temperature monitoring automatically cuts the laser and raises an alarm on contamination detection, preventing window shattering and optical damage.
Cutting Gas Pressure Monitoring
Real-time monitoring of assist gas output maintains cutting consistency when gas supply fluctuates, improving edge quality on thick carbon steel.
Smart Piercing
Monitors the piercing process and initiates cutting immediately upon confirmed pierce completion, improving efficiency and reducing over-piercing waste.
Auto Recut
Detects incomplete cuts and automatically re-runs the affected path segment, reducing scrap and operator intervention on demanding thick-plate jobs.
Smart Frame (Edge Detection)
Senses the plate edge position before cutting starts and adjusts the program origin automatically — eliminates manual edge-finding and mis-cuts on remnant sheets.
Variable Beam Shape
Switches between Gaussian (concentrated) and Homogeneous (uniform) beam distributions — optimising thin-plate speed or thick-plate piercing under the same laser power.
Cutting Path Monitoring
Monitors actual kerf width in real-time and adjusts focal position to compensate for thermal drift, ensuring consistent quality over extended production periods.
Real-time Focus Centering
Camera captures molten pool images continuously; coaxial beam deviation is detected and automatically corrected in real-time for stable cutting axis alignment.
Quick Focus Centering
One-button rapid calibration of beam-to-nozzle coaxiality, reducing setup time when changing nozzles or after a collision event.
Stray Light Monitoring
Detects back-reflected or scattered light from the cut zone that bypasses the protective window, triggering shutdown before internal optical components are damaged.
