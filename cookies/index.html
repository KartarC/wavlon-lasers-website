Cookie Policy

How Wavlon Lasers uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Wavlon Lasers Inc. uses them, and how you can manage your preferences. By using our website at wavlonlasers.com, you consent to our use of essential cookies. Non-essential cookies are only set with your explicit consent via our cookie banner.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and actions over time. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, and pixel tags, which work in comparable ways.

2. Cookies We Use

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. They cannot be disabled.

3. How We Obtain Consent

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner is displayed at the bottom of the screen. You may:

  • Accept All — enables essential, analytics, and marketing cookies.
  • Essential Only — enables only cookies required for site operation.

Your choice is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key wavlon_cookie_consent. The banner will not appear again unless you clear your browser data.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can update your cookie consent at any time by clearing the wavlon_cookie_consent key from your browser's localStorage, which will cause the banner to reappear on your next visit.

You can also manage cookies directly in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

To opt out of Meta advertising cookies, visit Facebook Ad Preferences or optout.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (e.g., financing partners, industry resources). These sites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which we do not control.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of any revised policy.
