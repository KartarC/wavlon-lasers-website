Our Story

Built in Canada.
Built for Manufacturers.

Wavlon Lasers was founded by people who understand the shop floor — engineers, machinists, and industrial sales veterans who saw a gap in the Canadian market for machines that are powerful, well-supported, and honestly priced.

The Wavlon Story

We Saw What Canadian Manufacturers Were Missing

For years, Canadian fabricators buying fiber laser cutters faced the same problems: machines shipped with no local service, sales reps who disappeared after the invoice, and support that meant waiting weeks for a technician who had to fly in from overseas.

We started Wavlon to fix that. Our team handles every sale, installation, training session, and service call from our Toronto base — no middlemen, no overseas call centres. When your machine needs attention, a Wavlon technician picks up the phone.

We carry machines from 1kW to 30kW+ — S-Series for job shops, P-Series for production lines, X-Series for heavy fabricators, and T-Series for tube and profile cutting. Everything is sourced from proven laser manufacturers and configured for Canadian electrical and safety standards before it ships to you.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Four principles guide every decision we make — from how we spec a machine to how we answer a service call.

Honest Specifications

We publish real-world cutting thicknesses, not theoretical maximums. If a machine cuts 25mm steel at quality, we say 25mm — not 30mm with an asterisk.

Real Local Support

Every service request is handled by our Canadian team. No overseas ticket queues. No guessing games. You have one contact who knows your machine.

Long-Term Partnership

We want to be your machine supplier for the next decade, not just one transaction. That means fair pricing, proactive maintenance reminders, and honest upgrade advice.

Continuous Improvement

We track every service case, part failure, and customer complaint. Patterns get fixed — in our machines, our processes, and our support. We improve every quarter.

Transparent Pricing

We quote all-in — machine, installation, training, and warranty — so there are no surprises on delivery day. What we quote is what you pay.

Customer-First Culture

We measure success by how many customers buy a second machine from us. Repeat business and referrals are the only metrics that matter in the long run.
