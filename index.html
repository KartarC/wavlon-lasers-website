Section 03 · Dual Exchange Table

Swap a sheet
in 35 seconds.

The WD-3015E ships with a vertically-stacked dual exchange table. While one pallet is in the cabin under the laser, the other is outside loading the next blank. When the cycle completes, the pallets swap top-to-bottom — the cut sheet drops, the loaded one rises, and cutting resumes with no operator intervention.

Section 04 · Cutting Head
BOCI · BLT310Manufactured by Bochu Electronic Technology — a Bochu Group brand.

The brain of
the beam.

BOCI's BLT family is the industry reference for high-power fiber cutting heads — scaled from 3 kW workhorses up to ultra-high-power 120 kW and 3D-capable variants. The BLT310 we fit to the WD-3015E delivers adaptive standoff, sealed modular optics, and field-serviceable lens cassettes. Scroll to take it apart.

Step 01 / 04 · Whole assembly

Compact, sealed, sensor-laden.

The BLT310 ships as a sealed assembly: collimator on top, focus module in the middle, capacitive sensor ring at the nozzle. Standoff is monitored at 1 kHz; the head can hold ±50 µm even as 6 mm stainless warps under cut load.

  • Power band3 — 15 kW
  • Focus stroke±25 mm
  • Standoff±50 µm
  • Sensor rate1 kHz
Step 02 / 04 · Mounting + Nozzle

Tool-free nozzle swap.

The mounting block carries the capacitive sensor, gas inlet, and the auto-positioning rails. Nozzles change in under five seconds via the quick-lock collar — no tools, no realignment, no test cuts.

  • Nozzle change< 5 s
  • Gas inletsN₂ · O₂ · Air
  • Max pressure25 bar
  • MountQuick-lock
Step 03 / 04 · Exploded view

Modular service internals.

Open the head and every consumable lifts out as an indexed cassette: cover slide, focus lens, collimator, beam splitter. Each cassette has its own seals — the rest of the head never sees ambient air.

  • Modules4 cassettes
  • Seal classIP55 / dry-N₂
  • Cover slideAuto-replace
  • Service time< 10 min
Step 04 / 04 · Focus optics

The lens is everything.

At the focus stage, a fused-silica plano-convex lens condenses the collimated beam to an 80-micron focal spot. The retainer is a precision-machined nickel-plated bezel that drops out of the cassette and re-seats to ±2 µm tilt accuracy.

  • Lens materialFused silica
  • Focal length150 mm
  • Focal spotØ 80 µm
  • Re-seat tol.±2 µm

Section 05 · HMI & Control

Cypcut.
Windows-native control.

The WD-3015E is driven by Cypcut, the de-facto Windows control suite for fiber laser cutting. CAD import, nesting, technology tables, live diagnostics — operators get a familiar Windows workflow on a 21.5″ portrait touchscreen, and engineers can drop into G-code when they need to.

  • Native CAD DXF / DWG / AI import, instant nest with material-aware spacing.
  • Technology tables Material × thickness × gas — every combination pre-tuned.
  • Live monitoring Cut quality, gas pressure, lens temp, beam dump — all on screen.
  • Network ready Drop files over LAN, queue jobs, push reports to MES.
