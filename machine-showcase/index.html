    Laser Cutting Head

    BLT Series by BOCHU

    Overview

    BLT Series Cutting Head by BOCHU Group

    Every Wavlon machine ships with a genuine BLT Series cutting head from BOCHU (BOCI brand) — one of China's leading precision optical equipment manufacturers, operating across the full laser power spectrum.

    Power Range 3kW – 120kW
    Brand BOCI / BOCHU Group
    Certification ISO 9001:2015
    High-Power Design

    Built for Continuous Industrial Use

    The BLT head is engineered for 24/7 production environments. Water-cooled optics maintain thermal stability at maximum power, while the quick-connect nozzle system keeps changeover under 90 seconds.

    Cooling Closed-loop water cooled
    Nozzle Swap <90 seconds
    Focus Adjust Motorized, ±10mm
    Internal Optics

    Precision Optical Architecture

    Inside the BLT head, a collimating lens, beam expander, and focusing lens work in concert to deliver a diffraction-limited spot. All optical elements are sealed against dust and humidity ingress.

    Beam Quality M² <1.1
    Optics Fused silica, AR-coated
    Sealing IP54 rated
    Lens Assembly

    Interchangeable Focal Lengths

    Swap between 125 mm, 150 mm, 200 mm, and 250 mm focal lenses to optimise kerf width and cut speed for each material thickness. Wavlon stocks all focal-length kits in our Toronto parts depot.

    Focal Options 125 / 150 / 200 / 250 mm
    Min Spot Size 50 µm
    Stock Toronto parts depot
