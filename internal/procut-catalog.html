Introduction

The ProCut Series is Wavlon's entry-level gantry platform — an enclosed cutting head and beam traveling over an open machine bed, built for fast, no-fuss sheet production.

Built on a welded, stress-relieved bed with an open work area, the WPC Series gives operators clear, unobstructed access to load and unload large or heavy sheet directly. The cutting head and cross beam run inside an enclosed gantry hood, while high-sensitivity light curtains and a built-in electrical cabinet keep the operator protected and the footprint tight.

The entry-level workhorse — from a 3015 frame to ultra-large-format UL beds. Designed for fast training. Backed by Canadian sales and service.

01 — OPEN BED

Open-Access Work Bed

The gantry travels over a fixed open bed, giving clear, unobstructed access to load and unload large or heavy sheet directly — no enclosure to work around.

02 — GANTRY

Enclosed Head & Beam

03 — SAFETY

04 — SUPPORT

Canadian-Backed Service

Sales, installation, training, and spare parts from a North American team — with remote support and locally available components.

Wavlon ProCut Series At a Glance 03 / 15
Highlight · 01 Open work area · enclosed cut

Open-Bed
Gantry Design.

A high-rigidity gantry carries the cutting head and cross beam across a fixed, open machine bed. With no surrounding cabin, the operator has clear access from every side — loading raw sheet and clearing finished parts is direct and fast, including large or heavy plate that would be awkward to feed into an enclosure.

Where it counts, the cut stays protected: the head and beam run inside an enclosed gantry hood with an OD-rated viewing window, and high-sensitivity light curtains halt motion before anyone reaches the cutting zone.

Cycle
01
Load onto the open bed

Sheet drops straight onto the open slat bed — crane, forklift, or by hand, from any side.

02
Gantry traverses & cuts

The enclosed head and beam track the program at up to 100 m/min while light curtains guard the zone.

03
Clear & reload

Finished parts and skeleton lift straight off the open bed for a fast turnaround to the next sheet.

Highlight · 02 Operator safety · light curtains

High-Sensitivity Light Curtains.

With an open bed, safety is built into the gantry itself. High-sensitivity light safety curtains screen the cutting zone and halt motion the instant the beam is broken, while a green status beacon signals machine state across the floor and the head and beam stay shielded inside the enclosed hood. Designed to meet North American safety requirements, including CSA standards.

Wavlon ProCut Series Highlight 02 — Light Curtains 05 / 15
Highlight · 02 · cont. Built-in cabinet · tidy footprint

Built into the body, organised for service.

The electrical cabinet folds into the machine body — no separate floor-standing enclosure to place or wire — with components laid out in clear tiers for fast diagnosis and service.

01 · Inside the cabinet

Built-in Electrical Cabinet.

Drives, source bay, breakers, and control electronics are arranged in clearly organised tiers behind a sealed door — every component visible and reachable for quick diagnosis. A full-height green beacon on the cabinet face reports machine status across the floor.

02 · Tidy footprint

Integrated, Compact Footprint.

Because the cabinet is integrated into the machine base rather than standing alongside it, the ProCut occupies less floor and installs with fewer external runs — power and control stay contained within the machine body, keeping the shop floor clear around the open bed.

Wavlon ProCut Series Highlight 02 — Electrical Cabinet 06 / 15
Highlight · 03 Rigidity · thermal stability

Engineered for Rigidity.

The machine bed is an in-house welded structure, annealed to release internal stress, with reinforced rib plates and mortise-and-tenon joints. The cross beam is high-strength steel, tempered for thermal stability under heavy cutting — so the ProCut holds dimensional accuracy through extended production.

Structure — At a Glance
01
Annealed welded bed

Stress-relieved welded structure minimises deformation for long-term accuracy and durability.

02
Reinforced rib plates

Internal mortise-and-tenon ribs add rigidity and suppress vibration at high-speed travel.

03
High-strength steel beam

Integrated, tempered steel cross beam holds accuracy under thermal load during heavy cutting.

04
Welded plate frame

Metal-plate welded construction forms a stable, rigid base for consistent long-term accuracy.

Steel slat work bed

Replaceable steel support slats carry the sheet across the open bed and swap out quickly once consumed.

Slag collection drawers

Pull-out trays beneath the bed catch dross and offcut for fast, low-labour cleanout between jobs.

Hardened guides & rack

Precision linear guides with helical rack-and-pinion drive carry the gantry smoothly at full speed.
