Applications

What Can a
Fiber Laser Cut?

Fiber lasers cut almost any metal — mild steel, stainless, aluminum, brass, copper, titanium, and more. Here's a breakdown of common applications and which Wavlon machine fits each one.

Common Applications

Parts & Components Cut by Wavlon Machines

Structural Brackets & Plates

Mounting plates, gussets, flanges, and connection plates in mild steel, stainless, or aluminum. High nesting efficiency cuts material waste.

S / P / X-Series

Chassis & Frame Components

Automotive and equipment chassis members, sub-frames, and cross-members. Tight tolerances on AHSS and boron steel.

P / X-Series

Enclosures & Panels

Electrical enclosures, control panels, instrument housings. Clean cut edges reduce deburring time before sheet metal forming.

S-Series

Tube & Profile Parts

Round, square, and rectangular HSS profiles with cope cuts, saddle cuts, and complex 3D end profiles for welded assemblies.

T-Series

Decorative Metalwork

Screens, grilles, signage, and art pieces in stainless, aluminum, and brass. Intricate filigree and small features cut cleanly.

S-Series

Heat Exchangers & Fins

Thin-gauge aluminum and copper fins and header plates for HVAC, refrigeration, and industrial heat transfer equipment.

S-Series

Pressure Vessel Shells

Thick carbon steel and stainless plate for pressure vessel shells, heads, and nozzle pads. X-Series handles plate to 80mm.

X-Series

Agricultural Wear Parts

Cultivator blades, discs, shims, and guards in AR400/AR500 abrasion-resistant steel. Fast turnaround for seasonal demand.

P / X-Series

Medical & Lab Equipment

316L stainless instrument trays, frames, and housings. Burr-free edges minimize post-processing on parts that go into sterile environments.
