    From the cutting head that touches the material to the drive system that moves it — every Wavlon machine is built with best-in-class components from industry-leading suppliers.

    Laser Heads & Controllers

    BOCI BLT Intelligent Cutting Heads

    The world's leading intelligent fiber laser cutting head — auto-focus, IP65/IP67, collision protection, variable beam shape, and closed-loop monitoring. BLT3 through BLT9 for 2D sheet cutting; BLT4T, BLT5H for tube; plane bevel and structural steel for 3D. Paired with Bochu FSCUT controllers and CypCut/HypCut software.
