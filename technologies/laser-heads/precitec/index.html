Why Precitec

Sensor-led cutting and serviceable optics

Precitec’s ProCutter family combines distance sensing, focus control, cooling and process monitoring in compact heads designed for industrial integration.

  • Stable, repeatable standoff control for demanding motion
  • Accessible protective optics for faster maintenance
  • Fieldbus and PLC integration through CutBox Pro
  • Flatbed, tube, profile, 3D and bevel configurations across the selected range
Wavlon integration

Configured as a complete system

A cutting head cannot be selected by laser power alone. Fiber interface, numerical aperture, controller integration, focal lengths, cooling and collision strategy all affect the final machine configuration.

Compatibility and availability depend on the selected Wavlon machine, laser source, power level and regional product configuration. Specifications may change; final order documentation governs.
