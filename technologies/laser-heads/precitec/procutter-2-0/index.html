Core advantages

Designed for Stable Industrial Cutting

Feature descriptions are summarized from Precitec’s current official product information and presented for configuration guidance.

01

Up to 85 kW

A high-load thermal and optical design supports industrial cutting configurations up to 85 kW.

02

Consistent cut quality

The optical and sensor platform is designed for around-the-clock cutting at high laser power.

03

Intelligent sensors

Integrated monitoring tracks critical components and process parameters for planned maintenance.

04

PierceTec support

Automated piercing control is designed to improve hole quality and reduce cycle time and rework.

05

Broad source compatibility

Precitec states compatibility with laser sources in the field; Wavlon validates the final source-head pairing.

06

Connected diagnostics

Status and error information can be surfaced through the ProCutter app and machine integration.
