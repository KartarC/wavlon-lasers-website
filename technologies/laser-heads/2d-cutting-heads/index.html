Variable Beam Shape — electronically switch between thin-plate and thick-plate beam profiles
Smart LaserOff — optimizes laser power at contour entry/exit for clean inner and outer edges
Collision protection standard
Protective window monitoring — temperature and contamination detection
Differentiation vs BLT4: Variable beam shape effectively doubles cutting efficiency when a single machine handles both thin (1–3mm) and thick (12–20mm) plate. Thin plate gets a smaller focused spot for speed; thick plate gets a wider profile for clean kerf and better gas flow.
