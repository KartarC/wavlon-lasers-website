BLT Laser Head
Selection Guide

Start with laser power, fiber specifications, material and production requirements. Then confirm the exact head, controls and machine configuration together. A component rating alone does not establish cutting capacity or compatibility.

For machine buyers

Buying a laser head or a complete cutting machine?

A laser head is one component of a cutting system. If you need a complete machine, compare the sheet or tube layout first, then specify the head as part of the written quotation. Wavlon ProCut is a machine series; Precitec ProCutter is a cutting-head family.

Compare component reference specifications, then confirm the exact head and optical configuration for your machine.

Step 1

Select by laser power

Use laser source power as an initial filter, then check the exact head variant against its manufacturer documentation. Confirm the fiber interface, optics, cooling, gas supply and control integration with the machine supplier.

Step 3

Specify the parts you need to cut

A thickness-to-power chart cannot confirm production performance on its own. Give the supplier representative parts and measurable acceptance criteria, then compare results on the proposed machine configuration.

RequirementWhat to include in the enquiry
MaterialGrade, surface condition and the thicknesses you cut most often, plus occasional maximum thickness.
Part geometryDrawing revision, holes, slots, contours and any edge or bevel requirements.
Finished-part acceptanceDimensions, tolerances, edge condition and downstream welding, painting or deburring needs.
Production workloadBatch size, part mix, shifts and handling time as well as cutting time.
Trial configurationMachine, head variant, source power, gas and agreed trial conditions recorded with the result.

Use the sample-cut worksheet to record comparable requirements. Confirm the trial scope and availability with Wavlon before sending material.

Step 4

Cutting gas selection

Specify the gas supply and expected edge condition in the trial and quotation. Confirm pressure, purity, flow and treatment requirements for the selected process; compare total operating cost rather than gas price alone.

Oxygen (O₂)
Ask about edge condition. Confirm whether the proposed oxygen process meets your part requirements and what cleaning or finishing is needed before the next operation.
Nitrogen (N₂)
Ask about consumption and supply. Request the nitrogen specification and estimated use for your actual parts, then assess supply capacity and downstream finishing on trial samples.
Compressed Air
Ask about the complete air system. Confirm compressor, drying, filtration and flow requirements. Have the supplier demonstrate the required edge quality instead of assuming air is suitable at a universal thickness limit.

Buyer questions

BOCI BLT or Precitec: what should buyers compare?

Compare the exact proposed variants and the complete machine package. Brand alone does not establish cut quality, compatibility, local stock or operating cost.

CompareAsk for written confirmation
Head and sourceExact model/revision, approved source power, fiber interface and optics.
Machine integrationMounting, focus and height control, controller interfaces, gas and cooling requirements.
Part performanceMeasured trial results for the same drawings, materials and acceptance criteria.
ConsumablesPart numbers, current prices, replacement assumptions and delivery times.
Support scopeWho handles diagnosis, replacement and warranty for your installation location.
Quoted configurationWhich items are included, optional or subject to engineering review.

Does a higher-power laser head mean the machine cuts thicker plate?

No. The head rating is a component limit, not a production result. Assess the source, optics, gas, controls and material together, then agree the required finished-part quality and cycle time in a cutting trial.

Can I choose any BOCI or Precitec head for a Wavlon machine?

Do not assume interchangeability. Ask Wavlon to confirm the exact head and integration in the machine quotation. For an existing machine, provide the machine and head model, serial information and source specification for a compatibility review before ordering a replacement.

Which head has the lowest running cost?

Compare current consumable prices, actual replacement intervals, gas use, maintenance and production results. A brand-wide claim of lower cost is not a substitute for a comparison based on your workload. Use the machine cost guide to include installation and operating expenses.

What should Canadian and U.S. buyers send for a machine quote?

Send material grades, usual and maximum thickness, sheet or tube sizes, representative drawings, production volume, installation city and purchase timing. State whether you are buying a complete machine or replacing a component. Start with the RFQ checklist if some details are still undecided.

Manufacturer reference: Precitec documents model-dependent optics and power ratings on its ProCutter 2.0 product page. These component specifications do not establish Wavlon machine availability. See Wavlon's ProCutter 2.0 reference and confirm the quoted variant. Buyer guidance updated October 15, 2026.

Choosing a complete cutting machine? Start with ProCut, PowerCut and UltraCut sheet laser layouts or TubeCut tube laser machines, then confirm the head, source and controls for that configuration. A head’s rated power is not a guarantee of machine cutting capacity.

Not sure which head is right for your application?

Tell us what you want to make and where the machine will be installed. Ask for a configuration review and a written machine quotation.
