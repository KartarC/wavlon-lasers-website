BCW600B Laser Profiler

The BCW600B is a laser scanning device that mounts on the cutting head gantry. It pre-scans the structural section before cutting and builds a real-time deformation model — compensating for bow, camber, and twist that would otherwise cause dimensional errors.

+Measures actual profile vs. nominal CAD
+Corrects cutting path in real time
+No manual measurement or shimming
+Critical for scallop and bolt hole accuracy
