FSCUT5000A — Heavy Tube

3-Chuck Heavy Tube 4-Chuck Configuration Zero-Tail Cutting 7-Axis Pulling Bevel Cutting H/U/L-Beam
3-chuck configuration for long heavy tube and structural profiles
Zero-tail cutting — minimizes material waste at tube end

FSCUT3000 Series

Entry and mid-range tube cutting controllers — FSCUT3000DE for standard 2-chuck, FSCUT3000A/D/H for small tube and single-chuck applications.

When to Choose FSCUT3000 vs FSCUT5000: The FSCUT3000 series is ideal for dedicated small-to-medium tube cutting lines where heavy structural profiles and bevel cutting are not required. For full structural profile range and bevel cutting, specify FSCUT5000A.
Compatible Machine

Configured for Wavlon T-Series

All FSCUT tube controllers ship pre-configured on Wavlon T-Series tube cutting machines. The FSCUT5000 series is standard on production tube lines; FSCUT3000 on entry-level configurations.
