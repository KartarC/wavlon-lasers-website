Plane Bevel Cutting Head

Purpose-built for flat sheet bevel cutting — produces weld-preparation joints directly from the laser without secondary operations. Works with FSCUT9100 controller.

Structural Steel Cutting

Structural Steel Cutting Head

3-in-1 structural steel, plane bevel, and 2D cutting from one head. The BCW600B laser profiler compensates for real-time deformation in H-beam and structural sections. Works with FSCUT9200 controller.

BCW600B laser profiler — real-time deformation compensation for H-beam and structural sections
3-in-1: structural steel cutting + plane bevel cutting + 2D sheet cutting
AB axis swinging for bevel cuts on structural profiles
Vision calibration — one-click calibration, no mechanical adjustments
Multi-function: scallop, deburring, bolt holes, and part marking
Professional nesting with Tekla integration and TubesT Structural Steel software
Works with FSCUT9200 controller for full automation

BCW600B Laser Profiler

The BCW600B scans the actual profile of the structural section in real time — compensating for bow, camber, and twist. Cuts remain precise even when the material deviates from its nominal dimensions.

3D Heads Need 3D Controllers

Plane bevel and structural steel cutting require the FSCUT9100 or FSCUT9200 controller — standard 2D controllers do not support 3D head kinematics.

For Plane Bevel

FSCUT9100

Vision calibration, bevel nesting with CypNest/Tekla, plate deformation compensation, V/Y/X/K bevel editing.
