Why BOCI BLT

Intelligent Cutting — Built Into the Head

BOCI BLT heads are not passive optics — they are intelligent sensor platforms that monitor, adapt, and protect in real time.

Auto-Focus

Capacitive height sensing with fast-focusing actuator maintains precise focal point automatically across varying material heights.

IP67 Protection

Full dust and water ingress protection — BLT heads withstand harsh cutting environments, metal particles, and cooling water splashes.

Collision Protection

Spring-loaded collision protection with protective screws — the nozzle deflects on impact instead of damaging the optics or machine.

Window Monitoring

Real-time protective window temperature and contamination monitoring — alerts before lens damage occurs, eliminating costly downtime.

Variable Beam Shape

BLT8 series switches beam profile between thin-plate and thick-plate modes — one head doubles efficiency across the full thickness range.

Closed-Loop Control

Integrated pressure and sealing sensors provide closed-loop feedback to the controller — smart piercing and auto recut are based on real data.

Factory-Matched
